## Other findings
- **Container limit.** With about 50 live probe sessions, the account stopped starting containers. `create_session` still returned success; the session just stayed `PENDING`. A scheduled firing reported `CONTAINER_LIMIT_REACHED`. The process must archive finished sessions promptly. See [19/container-limit](evidence/19/container-limit.md).
- **No write access at first.** See [blockers/github-write-refused](evidence/blockers/github-write-refused.md). Bob fixed it at 15:40Z.
- **Foreground sleeps are blocked.** In a probe, `sleep 90; echo slept` in the foreground was refused by the harness: `Blocked: sleep 90 followed by: echo slept. To wait for a condition, use Monitor ...` (row 11 evidence).
