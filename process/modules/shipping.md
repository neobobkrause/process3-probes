# shipping: requirements

Next number: 3

## Requirements

- **R1** [not yet met] The shipping cost of a parcel is 300 pence for the first 500 grams, plus 100 pence for each further 500 grams or part of it; the weight may be given in any unit the units module accepts.
- **R2** [not yet met] A parcel over 20 kilograms is refused with a RangeError that says the weight is over the limit.
