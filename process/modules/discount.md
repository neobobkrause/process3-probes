## Requirements

- **R1** [not yet met] The discount on an amount of whole pence at a percentage from 0 to 100 is the amount times the percentage divided by 100, rounded half up to a whole penny.
- **R2** [not yet met] An amount that is negative or not a whole number of pence, and a percentage that is not a finite number or is outside 0 to 100, are refused with a RangeError that names the bad value.
