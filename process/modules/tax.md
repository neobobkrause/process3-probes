## Requirements

- **R1** [not yet met] The tax on an amount in whole pence at the standard rate of 20 percent is the amount times 0.2, rounded half up to a whole penny.
- **R2** [not yet met] An amount that is negative or not a whole number of pence is refused with a RangeError that names the bad amount.
